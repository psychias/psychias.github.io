Under review2026

LLM Evaluation Discrepancies And How To Prevent Them

An audit of 46,559 evaluation records from 12 public sources (7,398 models, 1,954 benchmarks) that shows why leaderboard scores diverge. A controlled experiment measures how much the setup matters: prompt format alone drives 27% of the variance on GSM8K. The paper introduces EvalSpec, a reporting schema that makes evaluation configurations comparable.
